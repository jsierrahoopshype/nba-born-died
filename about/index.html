    Sources

    • HoopsMatic's NBA player database: the player list, season-by-season teams and games, points, rebounds and assists, height, position, nationality, championships and awards. Birth dates only when the source below has none.
    • HoopsMatic's NBA Career Map: birth and death dates and places (first precedence), All-Star selections, nationality, common names, and the country flags' country list.
    • Wikidata: the state, province or country of each birth and death place, common names such as nicknames, and the identity of players the sources above can't place (see below).
    • Basketball-Reference: the Hall of Fame flag.
    • Social Security Administration: cohort life tables for U.S. men (public domain), for the comparison on the lifespan page.
    • Headshots: HoopsMatic's NBA headshot collection. Flags: flag-icons (MIT license). Fonts: DM Sans and JetBrains Mono (SIL Open Font License).

    Living, deceased, status unknown

    • 5,106 players: 3,973 living, 1,129 deceased and 4 whose status is unknown. Data as of Oct. 10, 2026; ages on these pages are as of Oct. 10, 2026.
    • A player is living when a matched birth record has no death date and he would be under 100. He is never assumed living without a matched record.
    • Status unknown: no record could be matched with certainty, or no death is recorded but he would be 100 or older. When the regular sources can't place a player, Wikidata is checked for a basketball player born on exactly the same date with a compatible name; only a single, certain match is used. Unknowns are left out of living counts and the lifespan curves, and shown as their own number everywhere.
    • 17 death dates are known only to the month or year; ages at death for them are marked "about".

    Method

    • Players: everyone with an NBA or BAA regular-season or playoff appearance. ABA seasons are not included. Games are regular-season games.
    • Survival: Kaplan-Meier. Each player enters at the age of his NBA debut (never before 20); living players count until their current age. Curves stop where fewer than 10 players remain at risk.
    • Franchises follow the NBA's official franchise histories, including earlier names and cities.
    • These pages list dates and places only, never causes of death.
